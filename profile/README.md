## Continuous Waveform Insight for Critical Power Systems

Energy Quotient™ provides Continuous Point-on-Wave (CPOW) monitoring and physics-grounded analytics for mission-critical power systems in fabs, data centers, medical technology, defense, and utilities.

We turn continuous waveform data into real-time, explainable insight that operators can trust.

## Focus Areas

- Detailed power quality and root cause analysis
- Diagnostics for nuisance trips and unexplained equipment behavior
- Power resilience for mission-critical infrastructure
- Support for grid-connected and islanded systems

## Technology Approach

- **Hardware**: Precision sensor nodes designed for CPOW capture at 32 kHz
- **Edge Analytics**: Real-time diagnostics that keep data inside the facility boundary
- **AI + Physics**: Physics-grounded analytics and signal-literate AI for root-cause analysis
- **Integration**: Open, standards-oriented interfaces for EMS, SCADA, and IT systems
- **Security**: Secure-by-design with minimal exposed surface area

## How We Work

We run structured pilot deployments and forensic power quality investigations with operators who need deep visibility into real systems, not just averages and alarms.
